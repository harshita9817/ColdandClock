# Figure 1 legend

**Figure 1. Cold onset at dawn or dusk has distinct effects on freezing tolerance and elicits partly opposite transcriptional responses in sorghum.**

**(A, B)** Freezing tolerance of sorghum after one day of cold treatment [temperature and duration to confirm] begun at dawn (A) or dusk (B), measured by ion leakage. Relative injury is percent ion leakage scaled between the unchilled control at 0 °C (0%) and the fitted plateau of each sample's own group and experimental set (100%). Points show mean ± SEM of all samples at each temperature (two biological replicates in each of five experimental sets; n = 10). Curves are four-parameter logistic fits to the pooled samples of each group. In A, dark pink open circles are controls and light pink triangles are cold-treated plants. In B, navy open squares are controls and teal open circles are cold-treated plants. Insets show LT50, the temperature of 50% relative injury, calculated from a separate fit to each set (n = 5 sets; box, interquartile range; centre line, median; whiskers, minimum to maximum). Cold treatment did not significantly change LT50 after dawn onset (mean −2.22 °C in control, −2.17 °C in cold; P = 0.31; ns), and lowered it after dusk onset (mean −2.06 °C in control, −2.14 °C in cold; P = 0.046; *). P values are from one-way ANOVA followed by Dunnett's test against the control; with two groups these are equivalent to a two-sample t-test.

**(C, D)** Transitions of all 22,588 tested genes between differential-expression states across the time course after DAWN (C) or DUSK (D) cold onset. Time is shown as hours after cold onset; DUSK onset was 12 h after DAWN onset, so DUSK samples were collected 12 h later than DAWN samples at the same duration of cold. At each time point, each gene is upregulated (Up; light pink in C, light green in D), downregulated (Down; dark pink in C, dark green in D), or not differentially expressed (Not DEG; gray) relative to the time-matched control. Bar heights and numbers give gene counts in each state, and ribbons show genes moving between consecutive time points, coloured by their state at the earlier time point. Genes that were never differentially expressed are included in the gray bands (6,354 in C and 7,540 in D). DEGs were defined by DESeq2 as |log2 fold change| ≥ 1 and Benjamini–Hochberg adjusted P < 0.05, using three libraries per group, one from each of three growth trials, with growth trial as a model term.

**(E)** Numbers of upregulated and downregulated DEGs at matched durations of cold after DAWN onset (solid lines, circles) and DUSK onset (dashed lines, triangles).

**(F)** Overlap of DAWN and DUSK DEGs that change in the same direction at each matched duration, for upregulated (orange) and downregulated (blue) genes, shown as log2(observed / expected overlap). Expected overlap was calculated within dodeciles of mean expression and summed, to control for the effect of expression level on DEG detection. The dashed line marks the overlap expected by chance. Filled circles are significant in a two-sided dodecile-controlled test (Benjamini–Hochberg adjusted P < 0.05 across the 14 tests), and open circles are not.

**(G, H)** Log2 fold change of all 22,588 tested genes after cold onset, 11 h (G) and 15 h (H) after onset, shown as log2(Dawn/Control) on the x axis and log2(Dusk/Control) on the y axis, where each treatment is compared with the time-matched control by DESeq2. Colours show the DEG call in the two treatments: up after both (crimson; 653 genes in G and 768 in H), down after both (blue; 137 and 491), up after DAWN and down after DUSK (amber; 1,689 and 1,817), down after DAWN and up after DUSK (teal; 1,723 and 1,971), a DEG in one treatment only (dark gray), or not a DEG in either (light gray). Numbers in the panels give the counts of the four coloured groups. Values beyond ±8 are drawn at the axis limit.

**(I, J)** Percent of the genes in three selected Gene Ontology (GO) biological process terms that are DEGs after DAWN and DUSK onset, 11 h (I) and 15 h (J) after onset. Numbers in parentheses give the tested genes annotated to each term. Bars to the right show upregulated DEGs (light pink, DAWN; light green, DUSK), and bars to the left show downregulated DEGs (dark pink, DAWN; dark green, DUSK). The black tick on each bar marks the percent expected by chance, which is the percent of all tested genes that are DEGs in that treatment and direction. Stars mark significant enrichment (*, adjusted P < 0.05; ***, adjusted P < 0.001); significant depletion, where a bar falls short of its tick, is not starred. Enrichment used two-sided Fisher's exact tests in GOATOOLS against the tested-gene background, with Benjamini–Hochberg adjustment within each contrast and DEG direction.
