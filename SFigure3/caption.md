**Core circadian-clock transcripts retain onset information early and converge
unevenly during prolonged chilling.** Each row shows one clock or clock-associated
gene after cold treatment initiated near dawn (left) or dusk (right). Gray lines
show time-matched controls, colored solid lines show cold-treated leaves, and
colored dashed lines show the independently measured diurnal waveform mapped to
the present control-expression scale. Background shading denotes the 14-h light
and 10-h dark cycle of the cold experiment. T is one hour later than ZT.
